Describe the part. Get an accurate, printable model.

Formwerk is a desktop application for designing, generating and editing printable 3D models — local, no account, no subscription. An AI agent drives the same tools as the menus; geometry is computed by code, never by the language model.

Version 1.0 ships in 2026

For Windows and Linux. This page will be the download address — if you want an early look, reach out via the address in the legal notice.

Three ways to a part

Way 1 — Edit

Change a downloaded model

Drop the file, read the inspection report, click a face or a hole — then say in the chat what it should become, or pick from the context menu. Compare before/after, apply, export.

Way 2 — Design

Build new, with dimensions

Describe what you need. The agent creates parameters and places proven building blocks — threads, heat-set inserts, hinges. Turn the numbers, the model follows instantly.

Way 3 — Generate

From text or image

A generated mesh runs through the repair chain automatically, gets an inspection report and is split and pinned when needed — until it sits printable on the plate.

What's inside

  • Non-destructive history. Every step stays editable, every number can be changed later — nothing gets overwritten.
  • Layer analysis before slicing. Overhangs, islands, support volume and wall thickness before the slicer makes them expensive.
  • Fits and tolerances. Clearance, press fit, flush — from calibratable material profiles instead of rules of thumb.
  • Part library with standard parts. Screw holes, nut pockets, threads, magnet pockets — tested across their whole parameter range.
  • Too big for the bed? Auto split. Sectioning with alignment pins and automatically created fits.
  • Exact edges. B-rep kernel for chamfers, fillets and round-trip STEP.
  • Two colours without a second program. Labels raised, recessed, or as a colour change in a single 3MF file.
  • AI local or with your own key. Ollama on your own machine or an API key — and without either, everything except the chat keeps working.

What Formwerk is not

Better said up front:
  • Not a slicer. Formwerk analyses and prepares; the print file still comes from your slicer — Formwerk hands over directly and reads its G-code back as a cross-check.
  • Not cloud CAD. No accounts, no storage on someone else's server, no telemetry. Projects are files on your machine.
  • No dimensional guarantees from generated meshes. What comes from text or images is made printable — accurate design happens on ways 1 and 2.
