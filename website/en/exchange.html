What you built once, anyone can rebuild.

Your own part is a recipe in Solidon3D: a list of operations with their values. Whoever adopts it gets no finished shape but your steps — and can change every number in them.

What is traded here — and what is not

What others have shared

The exchange opens with version 0.2.3. The first part could be yours.

Share something yourself

In Solidon3D, Parts → Save as recipe creates the file. You upload it here — and get a mail with two links: one makes the part public, the other withdraws it again at any time.

It is never shown and serves only for confirmation. What happens to it is in the privacy notice.

The server checks every file before it appears anywhere: format version, length of the texts, licence, and whether each step names an operation that really exists. If something does not fit, you get all the reasons at once — not one after another.
