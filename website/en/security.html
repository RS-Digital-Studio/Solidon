Security and support

Report a vulnerability confidentially and see before purchase how long Solidon 1 receives security updates.

Report a vulnerability

Solidon 1 is a paid perpetual-use licence

The purchase price includes every security and feature update within major version 1. We provide security support and security updates for Solidon 1.x until at least 31 October 2031.

The version you purchased remains usable without a time limit afterwards. The end of the security-support period ends neither the licence nor access to your projects.

The time-limited demo has a 0.x version number and ends on 30 October 2026. It is not the paid version 1.

What to include in a security report

  1. Solidon3D version and operating system,
  2. the behaviour observed and its possible security impact,
  3. reproducible steps or the smallest practical test case,
  4. if known: the affected file, feature or dependency.

Do not send: licence keys, complete project files or personal data. If the investigation needs more, we will ask specifically for the smallest necessary evidence.

What happens next

We acknowledge receipt within two business days, assess impact and scope, and keep you informed about mitigation and disclosure. We do not treat good-faith research and coordinated disclosure as an attack.

For a demonstrably actively exploited vulnerability or a severe security incident, RS Digital promptly checks which statutory reports and deadlines apply to the specific case. Your route stays the same: one message to support@solidon3d.de is enough.

Scope

This route covers official Solidon3D packages from this website, local project processing, activation, update checks and support submission. Third-party slicers, Ollama, ComfyUI and external AI providers are not bundled. Still report any weakness in their integration that Solidon3D triggers or amplifies.
