Worum es geht

Solidon3D sitzt zwischen dem Modell aus dem Netz und deinem Slicer. Du klickst das Loch an und tippst den neuen Durchmesser ein, das übrige Teil bleibt, wie es war. Ohne so ein Werkzeug bleibt meist nur, die ganze Datei zu skalieren. Dann stimmt der Rest nicht mehr.

Vor dem Druck sagt ein Prüfbericht, was schiefgehen würde, etwa eine zu dünne Wand oder ein Teil, das nicht aufs Bett passt. Danach geht das Modell an deinen Slicer. Solidon3D läuft auf Windows, macOS und Linux, ohne Konto und ohne Cloud.

Die Demo wurde seit Ende August über 1.700-mal heruntergeladen. Seit dieser Spendenaufruf läuft, sind vier neue Versionen erschienen, zuletzt 0.4.4 am 19. September. Ansehen und laden: solidon3d.de

English

The hole in the STL you downloaded is 0.2 mm too small, and you don't have CAD? That's exactly what I'm building Solidon3D for, on my own. The demo is free. Please help me cover the running costs until sales can carry them. Sales start on 1 November.

What it does

Solidon3D sits between the model you downloaded and your slicer. You click the hole and type the new diameter; the rest of the part stays as it was. Without a tool like this you usually end up scaling the whole file, and then nothing else fits.

Before you print, a check report tells you what would go wrong, such as a wall that's too thin or a part that doesn't fit the bed. Then the model goes to your slicer. Solidon3D runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, with no account and no cloud.

The demo has been downloaded more than 1,700 times since late August, and four new versions have come out since this fundraiser started, most recently 0.4.4 on 19 September. Have a look: solidon3d.de/en/

Who I am

Robert Schneider, a trained electronics technician from near Bamberg, Germany. I do everything on Solidon3D myself, from the code to the manual to support. If you write to me, the person who wrote the code answers.

What it costs

Some of it I've already paid for myself: the Apple developer account, so the Mac version opens without a warning, and a code-signing certificate for Windows, so the Windows version will also start without a warning in future.

Every month:
• about €400 for two AI coding tools (Claude Max and ChatGPT Pro with Codex)
• plus business costs, web hosting and the domain

After fees, the €5,000 goal covers these costs for about ten months, into summer 2027, however fast sales pick up. I'm raising it in stages: €500 for one month first, then €1,500, €3,000 and €5,000. My own working time isn't part of the calculation.

If less comes in, I keep paying the rest myself and Solidon3D carries on. If more comes in, it lasts longer.

About the AI tools, since people ask: they help me write and check code. Inside the app, every piece of geometry is computed by code, not by an AI, and nobody needs the built-in chat to use Solidon3D.

What you get in return

Nothing, and that's deliberate. No reward, no discount, no credit towards a later purchase, no early access. I don't issue donation receipts, so please don't treat this as tax-deductible. GoFundMe doesn't allow rewards anyway.

What you do get: updates on this page, including where the money went.

From 1 November

Version 1.0 comes out as a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no account. Until 30 October the demo stays free and complete. Buying it is separate from this fundraiser.

You can help without money, too

Download the demo, drop in an STL that annoyed you, and tell me what didn't work. And if you like Solidon3D, share this page or mention it in your printing group. For a one-person project that often counts for more than the amount.

Thanks for reading!
Robert

Since 13 September four versions have come out, with 185 changes between them:

• 0.4.2 (15 Sep): Sketching gained polygons, slots and four new constraints. Large models are recognised up to thirty times faster.
• 0.4.3 (18 Sep): Organizers with compartments you can change one by one, hole patterns inside a drawn area, and a seal groove with a matching gasket.
• 0.4.4 (19 Sep): A spool in the filament storage can have up to four colours, and print suggestions for a large figure take seconds instead of minutes.

The Windows code-signing certificate is paid for. With it, the Windows version will also start without a warning in future.

There's now a YouTube series, “Make an STL fit”, with five tutorials in English and German. The first one shows how to enlarge and move holes in a finished STL without redrawing the part: https://youtu.be/FM1pPvrZZsw

I'm working on 0.5.0 right now. Resin printers get their own profiles, a clicked hole shows its dimensions right in the 3D view, and recognising large models gets a lot faster again. I'll name a date once it's done.
