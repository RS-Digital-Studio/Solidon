**English**

Hi everyone, here's a first report, ten days after the start. First of all, thank you to everyone who donated right at the beginning. You were quicker than I was with this update.

Since 13 September four versions have come out, with 185 changes between them:
• 0.4.1 (14 Sep): The Mac packages are now notarized by Apple and open with a double-click. You can round and chamfer edges on downloaded models too, and turn an existing hole into a slot.
• 0.4.2 (15 Sep): Sketching gained polygons, slots and four new constraints. Large models are recognised up to thirty times faster.
• 0.4.3 (18 Sep): Organizers with compartments you can change one by one, hole patterns inside a drawn area, and a seal groove with a matching gasket.
• 0.4.4 (19 Sep): A spool in the filament storage can have up to four colours, and print suggestions for a large figure take seconds instead of minutes.

The Windows code-signing certificate is paid for. With it, the Windows version will also start without a warning in future.

There's now a YouTube series, “Make an STL fit”, with five tutorials in English and German. The first one shows how to enlarge and move holes in a finished STL without redrawing the part: https://youtu.be/FM1pPvrZZsw

I'm working on 0.5.0 right now. Resin printers get their own profiles, a clicked hole shows its dimensions right in the 3D view, and recognising large models gets a lot faster again. I'll name a date once it's done.

I've reworked this page. The costs are listed more precisely now, above all the two tools I write the code with. The goal now shows the first stage, €500 for one month. The overall goal stays at €5,000.

What helps most right now: send this page to one or two people who print themselves.

Thanks!
Robert
